System Governance Policy

Manual journals affect General Ledger accounts directly. Do NOT use manual journals to modify stock quantities, customer balances, or supplier balances behind the scenes. Adjustments to those must go through Stock Adjustment, Customer Payment, or Supplier Payment modules. Once posted, journals are immutable and can only be reversed.
