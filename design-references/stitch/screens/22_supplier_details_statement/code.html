Accounts Payable Ledger Governance & Offset Protocol

Supplier balances are computed strictly from posted purchase bills, verified bank/cash payment vouchers, and approved return credit notes. Direct manual balance overrides are restricted by system compliance. In accordance with wholesale financial governance, supplier refund receivables are tracked independently to prevent unverified double offsets against pending merchandise liabilities.
